Allocate must spread an item over at most three zones. Once three zones have received a quantity, no further zone is used and whatever is still unplaced is reported in the OVERFLOW entry. Only zones that actually receive a quantity count towards the limit, so zones skipped for missing capacity, an empty name or cold-chain eligibility do not use it up. Allocations that need three zones or fewer are unchanged. Preserve all other behaviour and public APIs; edit only `AllocationCalculator.cs`.
